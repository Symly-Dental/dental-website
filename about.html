About us

About Symly Dental

Symly Dental is dental practice management software for UK dental teams. It is built by Symly Ltd., a software company based in Manchester, England.

What Symly does

Symly brings the working day of a dental practice into one browser-based workspace: appointment scheduling, patient records, clinical charting, treatment planning, billing and practice reporting. Reception, clinicians and practice managers work from the same patient record, so there is less hunting for information and more time for the patient.

Symly includes NHS treatment and English FP17 claim workflows. Live claim submission is subject to supplier acceptance and practice configuration, so ask us about current availability before relying on NHS submission.

Why we built it

Practice software is often a patchwork of systems that do not talk to each other. We built Symly so that a booking, a clinical note, a treatment plan and a payment all belong to one connected record, with staff permissions and audit records built in.
