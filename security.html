Security

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Symly protects the information that dental practices keep in it. Your practice is the controller of its data, and Symly processes it on your practice’s instructions.

Cyber Essentials

Symly Ltd is certified under Cyber Essentials, the UK government scheme that checks an organisation’s defences against common cyber attacks. The certificate was issued on 18 November 2026 and is due for renewal on 18 November 2027. It covers Symly’s own organisation and devices, not the providers that host the product.

Certificate number: 48152804-e3f8-4378-be2b-b4a59baa4462. Check the certificate on the official register. Download the certificate (PDF, 289 KB).

How we protect information

  • Connections to Symly use HTTPS.
  • Symly stores information with providers in the UK or the European Economic Area.
  • The database keeps each practice’s records apart.
  • Staff sign in with a single-use code sent to their email, or with a long password. Every staff account must also have an authenticator app, and no setting turns this off.
  • At the end of the contract, we prepare a complete export of the practice’s data, and a practice manager downloads it.

Reporting a security concern

If you think you have found a security problem in Symly, email support@symlydental.com. Tell us what you found and how to see it.

  • Do not access, change or delete information that is not yours.
  • Do not test against live practices or patient data.
  • Do not share the problem publicly until we have fixed it.
