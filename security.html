Security

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Dental records are among the most sensitive information there is. This page describes how Symly protects them, so your practice can review us as a supplier.

In summary:

  • Symly Ltd. is certified under Cyber Essentials.
  • Symly keeps information with providers in the UK or the EEA.
  • Each practice’s records are kept apart inside the database.
  • Staff and patients sign in with single-use codes or links, and staff can add an authenticator app.
  • A practice manager can download a complete copy of the practice’s data.

Cyber Essentials

Symly Ltd. is certified under Cyber Essentials, the UK government scheme that checks an organisation’s defences against common cyber attacks. The certificate covers the whole organisation. It was issued on 18 November 2026 and is due for renewal on 18 November 2027.

Certificate number: 48152804-e3f8-4378-be2b-b4a59baa4462. Check the certificate on the official register. Download the certificate (PDF, 289 KB).

Where information is kept

Your practice is the controller of its data. Symly processes it on your practice’s instructions.

Symly runs on providers that keep information in the UK or the European Economic Area. Our data processing agreement with your practice lists each one and what it handles. If your practice is thinking of joining, email support@symlydental.com for the list.

Encryption

Every connection to Symly is encrypted. When a practice works offline, the records downloaded to a device are encrypted too.

Signing in

  • Staff sign in with a single-use code sent to their email, or with a long password. Staff can also use an authenticator app on their phone as a second step.
  • Patients sign in to the portal with single-use emailed links, so they have no password to lose.
  • Practice tablets pair with one-time codes, and the practice can revoke a tablet at any time.

Keeping practices apart

Every practice’s records are kept apart inside the database itself. One practice can never see another practice’s records.

Who can see what

Each member’s role and permissions decide what they can see and change, and only practice managers can manage staff access.

Activity record

Symly records sign-ins, access to patient records and changes to them, with who did it and when. The apps cannot alter or delete this record, and practice managers can review it. The practice sets how long to keep it. It is also deleted with the rest of the practice’s data when that data is deleted on the practice’s instruction.

Documents and error reports

Patient documents are kept in private storage. Error reports have patient details removed before they leave Symly.

Artificial intelligence

We do not use practice or patient information to train artificial intelligence models.

How we build Symly

Every change to Symly runs automated security tests before it is released, and the code is checked for known weaknesses.

Leaving Symly

A practice manager can download a complete copy of the practice’s data at any time.

More detail

Our data processing agreement describes these measures in full. If your practice is thinking of joining, email support@symlydental.com for a copy.

Reporting a security concern

If you think you have found a security problem, email support@symlydental.com. Please do not test against live practices or patient data.
