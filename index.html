Ask us a question
Does Symly work in an internet outage?

Yes, for clinical work. If your internet or Symly goes down, you can still write notes, chart, update treatment plans and move booked appointments. Your changes upload when the connection returns. New bookings, payments, messages and documents need a connection. See how Symly works in an outage.

Do we need to install anything?

No. Symly runs in a web browser, so your practice needs no server. Symly Bridge is an optional small Windows app for the imaging computer.

Does Symly work with our X-ray software?

Symly Bridge works with any imaging software that saves to a folder. It sends new files to a holding area in Symly, and staff file each one under the right patient. Staff can also upload images and documents in the browser.

Does Symly support NHS claims?

Not yet. NHS claims are coming soon. Symly is built for the new NHS Dental Services Portal (NHSDSP), which replaces WebEDI, and sends English FP17 claims through its DPMS API. Ask us about the timing and your requirements.

How does Symly protect patient data?

Every connection to Symly uses HTTPS, and Symly runs on providers that keep information in the UK or the European Economic Area. Each practice’s records are kept apart, and roles and permissions decide what each member of staff can see. Symly records each sign-in and each access to a patient record. Read the security page for the detail.

How do we switch from our current dental software?

Tell us your current system, the records you need and the tools you rely on. Symly checks the imported data and gives you a report, and a practice manager approves it before you go live. We agree the training and support with you, and confirm the scope in your proposal.

Can we try our everyday workflows in a demo?

Yes. Bring the tasks that frustrate your team, such as changing a booking or checking a balance. We’ll walk through them with example records. Please don’t send patient information with your enquiry.
