Ask us a question
What is Symly Dental?

Symly Dental is browser-based dental practice management software for UK dental teams. The Symly software brings appointment scheduling, patient records, clinical charting, treatment planning, billing and practice reporting into one workspace.

Who makes Symly?

Symly Dental software is built by Symly Ltd., a company registered in England and Wales and based in Manchester. Read about Symly or email hello@symlydental.com.

Is Symly built for dental practices in Great Britain?

Yes. Symly is a dental practice management system built for practices in England, Scotland and Wales. It is designed around UK clinical and reception workflows, including NHS treatment and English FP17 claims, and the company behind it, Symly Ltd., is based in Manchester.

How does Symly handle appointment scheduling?

The Symly diary shows appointments, statuses and working hours for every clinician together, so reception can see who has arrived, which chair is free and when a clinician is next available. Switch between day, week, month and list views, track confirmations and arrivals, and plan around working hours, leave and blocked time.

How does Symly handle billing and payments?

Every charge, deposit and payment sits in the patient ledger alongside the treatment plan, so the balance question is answered from one screen. Symly supports payment plans for treatment paid in stages, and reports show outstanding balances, open treatment plans and overdue recalls so the team can follow up.

Can we try our everyday workflows in a demo?

Yes. Bring the tasks that frustrate your team: finding a note, changing a booking, checking a balance or building a recall list. We’ll walk through those workflows using example records, so you can judge how Symly fits. Please don’t send patient information with your enquiry.

How much does Symly cost?

Clear pricing starts with a clear quote. We’ll set out what’s included, the setup and training scope, ongoing charges and any additional costs, including email and SMS. You can review the full proposal before you commit.

Can we use Symly in a browser?

Yes. Symly’s practice workspace is available through your web browser. Existing users can sign in here.

What should we check before switching?

Tell us your current system, the records you need, and any imaging, payment or other tools you rely on. We’ll discuss migration options and compatibility, plus training and support arrangements, before you plan a move. Availability depends on your setup; confirm the agreed scope in your proposal.

Can patients book, complete forms and check in themselves?

Yes. In the patient portal, patients sign in with a secure email link to see their appointments, request a new appointment for reception to confirm, complete forms the practice sends and download shared documents. In the practice, a paired forms tablet collects medical histories, consent and treatment-plan signatures, and a check-in kiosk lets patients mark themselves as arrived with their date of birth and surname initial. Everything lands in the same patient record.

How do X-rays and scanned documents get into Symly?

Staff can upload images and documents to a patient’s imaging or documents tab in the browser. Symly Bridge, a small Windows app for the imaging computer, watches the folder your X-ray, scanner or camera software saves to and sends new files to a holding area in Symly, where staff file each one under the right patient. It works with any software that saves to a folder and never reads patient details. Ask us about availability.

Does Symly support NHS workflows?

Symly includes NHS treatment and English FP17 claim workflows. Live claim submission is subject to supplier acceptance and practice configuration. Ask us about current availability and your requirements before relying on NHS submission.

Can we discuss data and access requirements?

Yes. We can walk through staff permissions and audit records, and discuss hosting, backups and your contractual requirements as part of evaluating Symly for your practice.
