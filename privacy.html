Introduction

This is the privacy notice of Symly Ltd. We make Symly, the software that dental practices use to run their practice.

We are committed to being transparent about why we need your personal data and what we do with it. This notice sets that out. It also explains your rights when it comes to your data. “Processing” can mean collecting, recording, organising, storing, sharing or destroying data.

This notice is for four groups of people:

  • people who visit our website, symlydental.com;
  • people who contact us, for example to ask for a demo or a price;
  • people who act for a dental practice that is our customer, for example the person who accepts our contract and the billing contact;
  • Symly administrators, who sign in to the Symly admin portal to run the Symly platform.

For these people, Symly Ltd is the controller of the personal data. Each group has its own section below.

If you have any concerns or questions, please contact us at hello@symlydental.com.

Symly Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17162506. Our registered office is Piccadilly Business Centre, Unit C, Aldow Enterprise Park, Blackett Street, Manchester M12 6AE. We are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office, registration number ZC279999.

Patients and practice teams

This notice does not cover the information that a dental practice keeps in Symly about its patients and its team.

Each practice decides why and how it uses that information. In data protection law, the practice is the controller and Symly Ltd is its processor. We store and use the information only on the instructions of the practice, under a written contract.

This applies to every Symly app that a practice uses: the practice portal, the patient portal, the reception tablet, the check-in kiosk and the bridge. It includes dental records, staff records, accounts and sign-in records.

If you are a patient or a member of a practice team, ask your practice for its privacy notice. It explains how your information is used and how to use your rights. If you send a request about that information to us, we pass it to your practice and help the practice to answer it.

People who contact us

What data do we have?

If you contact us, we may process the following types of data:

  • your name and your email address, and your telephone number if you give it to us;
  • the name of your practice and what you tell us about it;
  • our correspondence with you;
  • a record of a complaint, or of a request about your data, and of our reply.

You do not have to contact us or give us this data. Please do not send us patient records or health information.

Why do we have this data?

We need this data to answer your questions, arrange a demo, discuss prices and follow up on your request. By law, we need to have a lawful basis for processing your personal data. We process your data because:

  • we have a legitimate interest in answering people who ask about our services. A legitimate interest is a business reason that does not override your rights;
  • you ask us to take steps before you enter into a contract with us yourself;
  • the law requires us to keep it or to disclose it, which is rare.

We do not rely on your consent.

Where do we process your data?

We collect this data from you, by email, by telephone or in a meeting. Our website has no enquiry form. The demo and contact links on the website open your own email application.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • the service providers named under “How do we store your personal information?”;
  • our professional advisers, or the authorities, if the law requires it or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Customer contacts

What data do we have?

When a dental practice becomes our customer, we keep details of the people who act for it. We may process the following types of data:

  • the name and email address of the person who accepts our contract for the practice, and the date;
  • the name, email address and telephone number of the billing contact;
  • the names and email addresses of the people we write to about the contract and the service, such as a practice manager;
  • our correspondence with you, including requests for support and complaints.

If you act for a customer, we need your contact details to run the contract.

Why do we have this data?

We need this data to make and manage our contract with the practice. We use it to set up the service, send bills, give notice of changes and planned maintenance, give support and handle complaints. We process your data because:

  • we have a legitimate interest in managing our contract with the practice that you act for;
  • it is necessary for our contract with you, if you are the customer yourself (for example a sole trader);
  • we have a legal obligation, for example to keep tax and accounting records.

We do not rely on your consent.

Where do we process your data?

We collect this data from you, or from another person at your practice. We do this by email, by telephone, in a meeting and when we set up the service.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • the service providers named under “How do we store your personal information?”;
  • our professional advisers, or the authorities, if the law requires it or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Symly administrators

An administrator is a person who signs in to the Symly admin portal to run the Symly platform.

What data do we have?

So that we can run the platform safely, we need to keep certain records about you. We may process the following types of data:

  • your administrator account: your name and your email address;
  • the sign-in links we email to you. Each link works one time, for 10 minutes, and we store it only in a scrambled form;
  • your sessions: when each session started, when you were last active, and the internet address and browser it came from;
  • an activity record of the changes you make, with the time. Examples are when you provision a practice, change billing or close a rights request;
  • the notes you write on usage visits, and the rights requests you log, recorded against your account;
  • error reports when something goes wrong. They go through the admin portal, so our error report provider does not receive your internet address, your cookies or your account details.

You must give us your name and your email address, because you cannot sign in without them. We do not record special category data about you in the admin portal.

Why do we have this data?

We need this data to let you sign in, to keep the platform and the practices on it secure, to show who changed what, and to find and fix faults. We process your data because:

  • we have a legitimate interest in running the platform safely and in keeping a record of what administrators do;
  • we have a legal obligation, for example to show how we handled a data rights request.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or make decisions about you by automated means.

Where do we process your data?

We collect this data from you and from your use of the admin portal. We share it only with:

  • the service providers named under “How do we store your personal information?”;
  • our professional advisers, or the authorities, if the law requires it or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Cookies in the admin portal

The admin portal uses one cookie, better-auth.session_token, to keep you signed in. The portal cannot work without it. It is set when you sign in. It ends when you sign out or when the session ends. On the live service its name starts with __Secure-.

Your browser also keeps one setting, theme, with your choice of light or dark mode.

The admin portal has no advertising, tracking or third-party cookies, so it does not ask you to agree to cookies.

How do we store your personal information?

Where we keep it

  • Emails. Hostinger provides our email service.
  • Customer records and administrator records. We keep them in our own administration system. Our providers of hosting, a database, email and error reports process them for us, only on our instructions. These providers keep the data in the UK or the European Economic Area.
  • The website. GitHub Pages hosts the website. Cloudflare provides its domain name service.

GitHub, Cloudflare and Hostinger may use infrastructure or service providers outside the UK. Their policies explain their processing and their international transfer arrangements:

  • GitHub’s privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
  • Cloudflare’s privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • Hostinger’s privacy policy: https://www.hostinger.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Hostinger’s data processing addendum: https://www.hostinger.com/legal/dpa

Where UK data protection law requires a safeguard for a transfer, the safeguard is an adequacy decision or an approved contract. An adequacy decision is a decision of the UK government that a country protects personal data well enough. An approved contract holds data protection terms that UK law accepts. Contact us for details of the safeguard that applies to your data.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries. If your practice does not become a customer, we delete your enquiry and our correspondence within 2 years of our last contact with you.
  • Customer contacts. We keep them for the length of the contract with the practice, and for 6 years after it ends. We need them for that time for tax and accounting records and in case of a legal claim.
  • Complaints and requests about your data. We keep the record for 6 years after we close it, to show how we answered.
  • Website hosting logs. GitHub keeps them under its own retention arrangements.
  • Administrator sign-in links. A sign-in link stops working after 10 minutes, or when you use it.
  • Administrator sessions. A session ends 12 hours after you sign in, after 30 minutes without activity, or when you sign out. Nothing extends the 12 hours. We delete the record of a session when you sign out. We delete the record of an ended session the next time its browser opens the admin portal. If that browser does not open the admin portal again, the record stays until we delete it by hand.
  • Administrator activity record. The admin portal cannot change or delete the activity record. We keep it for 6 years after your account ends. Then we delete it by hand.
  • Notes on usage visits. We keep a note until we delete the data of its practice.
  • Administrator accounts. We keep your account until we remove it, when you no longer administer the platform.

How we dispose of it

When the time above ends, we delete the data from our email and from our systems. Copies in the backups of our providers expire on the schedule of each provider.

Our website

The website sets no cookies. It has no advertising, no analytics tracking and no enquiry form.

To deliver the website and protect it from attacks, GitHub Pages receives the internet address (IP address) of each visitor. We do not receive these addresses and we do not use them to identify you.

Your rights

The data that we keep about you is your data. We keep it confidential and we use it appropriately. You have the following rights when it comes to your data:

  • You have the right to request a copy of all of the data we keep about you. We do not charge for this.
  • You have the right to ask us to correct any data we have which you believe to be inaccurate or incomplete. You can also request that we restrict all processing of your data while we consider your request.
  • You have the right to ask that we erase any of your personal data which is no longer necessary for the purpose we originally collected it for.
  • You may also request that we restrict processing if we no longer require your personal data for the purpose we originally collected it for, but you do not wish for it to be erased.
  • Where we process your data as part of our legitimate interests, you have the right to object to that processing. We will restrict all processing of this data while we look into your objection.
  • Where we process your data for a contract with you, you can ask for a copy in a form that you can give to another organisation.

These rights depend on the circumstances, and the law sets some limits on them. To use a right, email hello@symlydental.com. We may ask you for information to confirm who you are, so that we do not give data to the wrong person. We will respond as soon as possible, and at the latest within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we use your data, please tell us first, so that we can put it right. Our complaints procedure is on symlydental.com/legal.html. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office:

  • Information Commissioner’s Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF
  • https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint

Changes to this notice

We update this notice when the way we use personal data changes. The date at the top shows when we last updated it.
